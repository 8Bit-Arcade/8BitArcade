PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: December 2024

1. INTRODUCTION

8-Bit Arcade ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our gaming platform and services.

By using 8-Bit Arcade, you consent to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use our services.

2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Wallet Address: Your public blockchain wallet address when you connect to our platform
  • Username: Optional display name you choose for leaderboards
  • Transaction Data: Records of token purchases and tournament entries
  • Support Communications: Messages you send through our community channels

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Game Data: Scores, gameplay statistics, and session information
  • Device Information: Browser type, operating system, and device identifiers
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, features used, and interaction patterns
  • IP Address: For security and fraud prevention purposes
  • Cookies: Session cookies for platform functionality

2.3 Blockchain Data

All blockchain transactions are publicly visible on Arbitrum One. This includes wallet addresses, transaction amounts, and smart contract interactions. This is inherent to blockchain technology and is not controlled by us.

Privacy Note: We do not collect personal identifying information such as your name, email address, or physical address unless you voluntarily provide it through support channels.

3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use collected information to:

Purpose Data Used
Provide gaming services Wallet address, game data
Display leaderboards Username, scores, wallet address (partial)
Process rewards & prizes Wallet address, game scores
Detect cheating & fraud Game data, IP address, usage patterns
Improve the platform Aggregated usage data, analytics
Respond to inquiries Support communications
Comply with legal obligations As required by law

4. DATA SHARING & DISCLOSURE

4.1 Public Information

The following information is publicly visible:

  • Leaderboard rankings with usernames and scores
  • Tournament results and prize distributions
  • Blockchain transactions (wallet addresses, amounts)

4.2 Third-Party Services

We may share data with:

  • Firebase (Google): Database hosting and authentication
  • Blockchain Networks: Arbitrum One for transaction processing
  • Analytics Providers: Aggregated, anonymized usage data
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or court order

4.3 We Do NOT

  • Sell your personal information to third parties
  • Share your data for advertising purposes
  • Provide your information to data brokers

5. DATA SECURITY

We implement security measures including:

  • HTTPS encryption for all data transmission
  • Firebase Security Rules for database access control
  • Rate limiting to prevent abuse
  • Regular security reviews and updates

However, no system is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information. You are responsible for securing your own wallet and private keys.

6. DATA RETENTION

  • Game Data: Retained indefinitely for leaderboard history
  • Transaction Records: Retained for legal and accounting purposes
  • Usage Analytics: Retained in aggregated form for 2 years
  • Support Communications: Retained for 1 year after resolution

Blockchain data is immutable and cannot be deleted. This includes all on-chain transactions, wallet addresses, and smart contract interactions.

7. YOUR RIGHTS

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data (where technically possible)
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format
  • Objection: Object to certain data processing
Limitations: We cannot delete blockchain data, leaderboard history that has been publicly displayed, or data required for legal compliance.

To exercise these rights, contact us through our community channels.

8. COOKIES & TRACKING

We use essential cookies for:

  • Maintaining your wallet connection session
  • Remembering game preferences
  • Platform security and fraud prevention

We do not use tracking cookies for advertising purposes. You can disable cookies in your browser, but some Platform features may not function properly.

9. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

8-Bit Arcade is not intended for users under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us for removal.

10. INTERNATIONAL USERS

Our services are operated globally. Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries with different data protection laws than your country of residence. By using our services, you consent to such transfers.

11. CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be announced through our community channels. Your continued use after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
