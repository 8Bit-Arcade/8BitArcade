PAUSE / UNPAUSE

Temporarily halt all purchases without ending the sale. Use during maintenance or market events.

EXTEND SALE

Add additional time to the sale end date. Cannot shorten the sale.

WITHDRAW FUNDS

Transfer all ETH and USDC from the contract to a recipient address. Does not touch 8BIT tokens.

FINALIZE SALE

Permanently ends the sale and burns all unsold 8BIT tokens. Only callable after the sale end time. This cannot be undone.

After finalizing, you can distribute tokens to buyers via the Token Distribution tab.

ETH PRICING
