## Tournament Overview

### What Are Tournaments?

**Tournaments** are time-limited competitions with:
- 💰 **Entry fees** (paid in 8BIT tokens)
- 🏆 **Large prize pools** (paid in 8BIT)
- ⏰ **Set duration** (hours to weeks)
- 👥 **Limited participants** (or unlimited)
- 🎮 **Specific game** focus

**Key Difference from Daily Rewards:**
- Daily rewards = FREE, smaller prizes
- Tournaments = Paid entry, MUCH larger prizes

## How Tournaments Work

### Entering a Tournament

1. **Browse Tournaments**
   - Visit **[play.8bitarcade.games/tournaments](https://play.8bitarcade.games/tournaments)**
   - See all active and upcoming tournaments
   - Filter by entry fee, game, prize pool

2. **Select Tournament**
   - Click on tournament you want to join
   - Review details:
     - Entry fee
     - Prize breakdown
     - Duration
     - Current participants
     - Game rules

3. **Pay Entry Fee**
   - Click **"Enter Tournament"**
   - Approve 8BIT token spend in MetaMask
   - Confirm transaction
   - Entry confirmed!

4. **Play Games**
   - Play the specified game
   - Submit scores during tournament period
   - Unlimited attempts (only best score counts)
   - Track your ranking in real-time

5. **Tournament Ends**
   - Final rankings locked
   - Prizes distributed automatically
   - Rewards sent to winner wallets
   - Results published

### Tournament Phases

```
Registration → Active Competition → Results → Payout
    ↓                ↓                  ↓         ↓
Entry fees      Play games         Rankings    Winners
collected       Submit scores      finalized   receive 8BIT
```

### Scoring System

**Highest score wins:**
- Only your BEST score counts
- Play unlimited times
- Improve throughout tournament
- Last-minute pushes allowed

**Tiebreakers:**
- First to achieve score ranks higher
- Encourages early competition
- Fair and transparent

## Prize Distribution

### How Prize Pools Are Funded

**50/50 Split of Entry Fees:**

**Example: 100 players × 10,000 8BIT entry = 1,000,000 8BIT collected**

- **50% (500,000 8BIT)** → Prize pool
  - Distributed to winners
  - Based on ranking position

- **50% (500,000 8BIT)** → Burned
  - Sent to burn address
  - Reduces supply permanently
  - Creates deflationary pressure

## Tournament Strategies

### Strategy 1: Selective Entering

**Don't enter every tournament:**
- Choose games you're best at
- Enter when prize pool justifies risk
- Skip tournaments with too many participants
- Focus on events with less competition

### Strategy 2: Early Bird Advantage

**Enter and play early:**
- Set the pace with high score
- Psychological pressure on others
- More time to defend position
- See competition develop

### Strategy 3: Last-Minute Clutch

**Wait until final hours:**
- See what score you need to beat
- One perfect run can win
- Less time for others to respond
- High-pressure, high-reward

### Strategy 4: ROI Focus

**Calculate expected value:**

Example:
- 10,000 8BIT entry fee
- 100 participants = 500,000 8BIT prize pool
- You estimate 20% chance to place top 3
- Expected value = (Prize if win × Probability) - Entry fee

**Only enter when EV is positive.**

### Strategy 5: Practice First

**Before entering:**
- Play game extensively in free mode
- Know your skill ceiling
- Understand what score is competitive
- Enter only when confident
