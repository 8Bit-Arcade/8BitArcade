# PVP Tournaments (Brackets)

## Head-to-Head Bracket Competition

**PVP Tourneys** are bracket-style elimination tournaments where players compete directly against each other, one match at a time.

> **Navigation:** Top nav **PVP → Tourneys** or go directly to **`/blitz/brackets`**

---

## PVP vs Blitz Mode

| Feature | Blitz Mode (`/blitz`) | PVP Tourneys (`/blitz/brackets`) |
|---------|----------------------|----------------------------------|
| Format | Score leaderboard | Head-to-head bracket |
| Opponents | Everyone | Direct 1v1 matchups |
| Structure | Free play, best score | Elimination rounds |
| Style | Casual competitive | Esports-style |

---

## PVP Nav Structure

The **PVP** menu in the nav bar contains two modes:

| Sub-item | URL | Description |
|----------|-----|-------------|
| Arena 1v1 | `/multiplayer` | Live 1v1 matches with real-time scoring |
| Tourneys | `/blitz/brackets` | Bracket-style elimination tournaments |

---

## How Bracket Tourneys Work

1. **Browse open brackets** at `/blitz/brackets`
2. **Join a bracket** before it fills
3. **Compete in rounds** — win to advance, lose to exit
4. **Final winner** takes the prize pool

### Bracket Types

- **Single Elimination** — one loss ends your run
- **Double Elimination** — one loss moves you to the lower bracket

---

## Arena 1v1

For instant head-to-head action without bracket scheduling, use **Arena 1v1** (`/multiplayer`):

- Join an open match lobby or create your own
- Wager 8BIT tokens against your opponent
- Highest score at end of match wins
- Results recorded on-chain

---

## Getting Started with PVP

1. **[Connect Your Wallet](../getting-started/connect-wallet.md)**
2. Navigate to **PVP → Arena 1v1** for instant matches
3. Navigate to **PVP → Tourneys** for bracket competitions
4. Win 8BIT tokens and climb the leaderboard

---

*PVP features require a connected wallet. Wagers are locked in smart contracts and released automatically to the winner.*
